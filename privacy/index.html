Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Overview

ChatterMost helps users view and manage previews of their own social media videos in one dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access and how it is used.

Information We Access

When you connect your TikTok account, ChatterMost may access basic profile information and public video metadata that you authorize through TikTok, such as your display name, avatar, video IDs, titles, descriptions, cover images, embed links, share links, duration, and creation time.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to display your TikTok video previews and related metadata inside ChatterMost. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

ChatterMost may store access tokens and video metadata needed to provide the app functionality. Temporary media URLs from TikTok may expire and may be refreshed through TikTok APIs when needed.

Sharing

We do not share your TikTok data with third parties except as required to operate the app, comply with law, or protect the security of the service.

Your Choices

You can revoke ChatterMost's access to your TikTok account at any time in your TikTok account settings. You may also request deletion of stored data by contacting us.

Contact

If you have questions or deletion requests, contact us at chattermost@webea.ch.
